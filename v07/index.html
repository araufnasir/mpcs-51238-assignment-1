What does a price change do?

Buyers react to price. How much they react is called elasticity. The two lines below show the same price rise hitting two different products.

Big

Elastic

Buyers react a lot. Easy to skip or swap, so a price rise can lower total income.

Small

Inelastic

Buyers barely react. Hard to do without, so a price rise can raise total income.

Reasons

Substitutes, necessity and share of budget decide which kind a product is.
