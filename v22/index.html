What does a price change do to your product?

Pick something close to what you sell or buy, then change the price, the cost and how easy it is to replace. See who stays, who leaves, and whether you earn more.

What is elasticity?

How strongly buyers react when the price moves. Big reaction: elastic. Small reaction: inelastic.

Why does it differ?

Alternatives to switch to, whether it is a need or a wish, and how big it is in the budget.

Why does it matter?

It tells you whether a price rise brings in more money or just drives customers away.
