Mia’s Cookie Stand

Mia sells cookies. One day she wonders: what happens if I change the price?

💸⬆️🙁

Price goes up…

Mia charges more. Some of her friends say, “Hmm, maybe not today.” When prices go up, fewer people buy.

🍪🍩🍰

Lots of other treats!

Friends can buy a donut or a cake instead. Lots of friends leave. We say the cookies are elastic: buyers stretch away from the price.

🚗⛽

But not everything!

Mia’s dad needs petrol to drive to work. The price goes up, and he still buys it. We say petrol is inelastic: buyers barely budge.

🤔🔁🛒💰

Why are they different?

Can I swap it for something else? Do I really need it? Is it a big part of my money? The answers tell us how much people react.

💡

Mia’s big idea

Before changing a price, Mia asks: “If this cost more, what would my customers do instead?”

That’s called price elasticity. The end!
