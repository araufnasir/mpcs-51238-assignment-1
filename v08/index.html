Elasticity

That's the fancy word for "how much buyers react to price." That's all it is!

Shop smart: 3 things that decide it!

  • SUBSTITUTES: a cheaper twin on the next shelf? Buyers switch!
  • NECESSITY: can't live without it? Buyers pay!
  • BUDGET SIZE: big-ticket items make shoppers hunt for deals!

Why you should care!

Raise an elastic price and your income can DROP. Raise an inelastic price and it can CLIMB. Before you change a price, ask: "What would my customers do instead?"
