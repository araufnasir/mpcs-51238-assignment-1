Price change. Demand moves.

THIS IS NOT OPINION. WHEN THE PRICE GOES UP, PEOPLE BUY LESS. THE ONLY QUESTION IS HOW MUCH LESS.

That "how much" is elasticity.

ELASTICITY = HOW HARD BUYERS REACT WHEN THE PRICE MOVES. NOTHING MORE. NO MATH REQUIRED.

ElasticBUYERS FLEE. CAKE. CONCERT TICKETS. ANYTHING YOU CAN SKIP OR SWAP.
InelasticBUYERS STAY. PETROL. MEDICINE. ELECTRICITY. ANYTHING YOU NEED.

Why you should care.

  1. RAISE THE PRICE OF AN ELASTIC THING AND YOU LOSE MORE IN SALES THAN YOU GAIN IN MARGIN.
  2. RAISE THE PRICE OF AN INELASTIC THING AND THE CUSTOMERS GRUMBLE, THEN PAY.
  3. SUBSTITUTES MAKE DEMAND ELASTIC. NECESSITY MAKES IT STICKY. BUDGET SIZE AMPLIFIES BOTH.

Ask one question.

"IF THIS COST MORE, WHAT WOULD MY CUSTOMERS DO INSTEAD?" LEAVE? THEN DO NOT RAISE IT. PAY? THEN YOU HAVE ROOM.
