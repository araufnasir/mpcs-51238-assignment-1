A one-minute guide

What does a price change actually do?

When something costs more, people buy less. How much less depends on the product, and that difference has a name: price elasticity.

Try it ↓

1Run a lemonade stand

Pick a situation, drag the price up, then switch to the other one.

Same price rise, different reaction. How strongly buyers react to price is price elasticity. A big reaction (rival next door) is elastic. A small one (only stand at the festival) is inelastic.

2Why do some products react more?

🔁

Alternatives

If something else will do, buyers switch quickly.

🏠

Need or wish

Needs hold up. Wishes get dropped when prices rise.

💰

Size of the bite

Big purchases make people shop around.

3Why it matters

Raise an elastic price and you can earn less. Raise an inelastic price and you can earn more. Knowing which one you have is the difference between a smart price rise and an expensive mistake.

Optional: try your own product

Before changing a price, ask: if this cost more, what would my customers do instead?
