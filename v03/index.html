DEMAND TERMINAL: what does a price change actually do?

Every price move gets a reaction from buyers. How big that reaction is has a name: price elasticity. Big reaction = elastic. Small reaction = inelastic.

Live simulator

Reading the board

Sold = how many units customers still buy. Revenue = price × units sold, the money actually coming in.

Elastic items: raising the price loses so many buyers that revenue falls. Inelastic items: most buyers stay, so revenue rises.

Why they differ: substitutes available, necessity vs. luxury, and share of your budget.
