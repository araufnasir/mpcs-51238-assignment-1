Definition

ELASTICITY

How strongly buyers react when the price moves. Roughly: the % drop in sales for each 1% rise in price.

Above 1: elastic, buyers react a lot. Below 1: inelastic, buyers barely react.

Drivers

Substitutes: easy alternatives raise elasticity.

Necessity: needs lower it, wants raise it.

Budget share: bigger purchases raise it.

Why it matters

Elastic: price rises can cut revenue.

Inelastic: price rises can lift revenue.

Ask: "If this cost more, what would my customers do instead?"
