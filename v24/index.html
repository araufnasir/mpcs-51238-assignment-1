What does a price change actually do?

When something costs more, people buy less. How much less depends on the product, and that difference is called price elasticity.

Try it: run a lemonade stand

Same price rise, different reaction. That is price elasticity: how strongly buyers react to price.

The same 10% rise, different results

Cake buyers can skip or swap, so many leave and income falls. Petrol buyers still need to drive, so most stay and income rises.

Why do some products react more?

🔁

Alternatives

If something else will do, buyers switch quickly.

🏠

Need or wish

Needs hold up. Wishes get dropped when prices rise.

💰

Size of the bite

Big purchases make people shop around.

Words in plain English

Demand
How much of something people want to buy at a given price.
Price elasticity
How strongly demand reacts when the price changes.
Elastic
Buyers react a lot.
Inelastic
Buyers barely react.
Money in
Price times number sold (economists say revenue).

Before changing a price, ask: if this cost more, what would my customers do instead?
