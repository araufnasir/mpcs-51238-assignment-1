What does a price change do?

1. Price up, buyers down.

Slide up the curve and fewer people buy.

2. How steep?

Steep curve = inelastic: buyers barely react (petrol). Flat curve = elastic: buyers bolt (cake).

3. Why?

Substitutes, need vs. want, and share of budget.

Before changing a price, ask: "What would my customers do instead?"
