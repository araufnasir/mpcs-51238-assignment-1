What's going on? How much buyers react to price is called price elasticity. At a festival with no rivals, people are thirsty and have nowhere else to go, so demand is inelastic: charge more. Next to a rival stand, anyone can walk ten steps and buy cheaper, so demand is elastic: raising your price empties your queue.
