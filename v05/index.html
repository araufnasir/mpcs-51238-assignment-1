Elasticity & Co.

Your plain-language receipt
"What does a price change do?"

1 PRICE RISE+10%

You raise a price by one tenth. Simple.

1 BUYER REACTION???

Demand always reacts: higher price, fewer buyers. How many fewer is the real question.

1 ELASTICITYFREE

The name for how strongly buyers react. Big reaction: "elastic". Small reaction: "inelastic".

PETROL (inelastic)-2%

Sales dip a little. People still need to drive. Income goes UP.

CAKE (elastic)-25%

Easy to skip or swap. Sales fall faster than price rose. Income goes DOWN.

SUBSTITUTES?x1

Alternatives nearby make buyers leave quickly.

NECESSITY?x1

Can't do without it? Buyers stay.

BIG % OF BUDGET?x1

Large purchases make people shop around.

TOTAL TAKEAWAY1 Q

Before changing a price ask: "If this cost more, what would my customers do instead?"
